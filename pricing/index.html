A few straight answers

Can I pay today?

Not yet. This page is a preview of proposed prices. Buttons will not take a card or collect payment. The calculator and worksheet are free now.

Do I have to link my bank?

No. Budget App is manual-first. You enter balances and log payments yourself.

What’s free, and what is Plus for?

The calculator, debt-free date, basic tracker, journey path, and basic milestones stay free. Plus is for staying on the plan: streak reminders, shareable milestone cards, an accountability partner, cloud sync, and what-if planning.

What if Plus isn’t a fit later?

When checkout opens, you’ll get a 30-day trial and a 30-day money-back promise after the first charge. Prices here may still change.
